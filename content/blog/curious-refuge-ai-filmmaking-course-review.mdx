## Quick Verdict

- **What it is:** A structured 4-week bootcamp (pre-recorded, cohort-paced) with feedback from industry professionals, a community, templates, and a completion certificate.
- **Price:** $749 per artist for the single AI Filmmaking course, or $149 per month for the All Access Membership (listed prices at time of checking).
- **Best for:** Creators who want guided weekly accountability, peer community critique, and a recognized credential, and are comfortable with the premium price point.
- **Maybe not for:** Solo creators on a tight budget, or those who want lifetime on-demand access at their own schedule without monthly cohort deadlines.
