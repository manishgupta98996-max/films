## The AI Filmmaking Production Pipeline

| Stage | What You Need | Recommended Industry Tools |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Ideas & Script** | Story, screenplay, scene breakdown, shot lists | **Claude 3.5 Sonnet**, ChatGPT |
| **2. Characters & Locations** | Consistent still plates to animate | **Midjourney v6**, **Flux 1.1 Pro**, Higgsfield |
| **3. Video Generation** | Moving cinematic shots from images or prompts | **Runway Gen-3 Alpha**, **Kling AI**, Seedance |
| **4. Voice & Dialogue** | Emotional character dialogue and narration | **ElevenLabs** |
| **5. Music & Score** | Scene score, atmospheric cues, and mood tracks | **Suno v3.5**, Udio |
| **6. Editorial & Color** | Assembling pacing, color grade, and master cut | **DaVinci Resolve**, Premiere Pro, CapCut |

## 1. Script and Ideas: Claude 3.5 Sonnet

Use a large language model to brainstorm premises, outline three acts, draft scenes, and format a script for production. The most useful habit is to iterate: ask for a beat sheet first, then scenes, then dialogue.

- **Good for:** Story development, scene-by-scene breakdowns, shot lists, and script formatting.
- **Watch out for:** Generic plots. Give it your own characters, tone, sensory details, and narrative constraints.

---

## 2. Characters and Locations: Midjourney & Flux

Consistent characters and settings are the hardest part of AI film. You generate high-resolution reference images first, then animate them using image-to-video.

- **Midjourney & Flux:** Industry-standard generators for photorealistic concept plates, turnaround sheets, and lighting reference.
- **Higgsfield:** Commonly described as strong for dramatic camera moves and VFX-style shots.
- **LTX Studio:** Built around the story rather than a single shot, offering script-to-storyboard pre-visualization.
- **Watch out for:** Characters drifting between shots. Save reference sheets and reuse exact physical descriptors.

## 3. Video Generation: Runway & Kling AI

- **Runway Gen-3 Alpha:** A comprehensive creative toolset around video models, including motion brushes, camera controls, and inpainting features.
- **Kling AI & Seedance:** High-fidelity video generation models with exceptional physics and realistic camera motion.
- **Watch out for:** Short clip lengths, unnatural morphing, and credit-based pricing that burns fast. Test a few shots on trial tiers before committing to an enterprise plan.

---

## 4. Voice & Dialogue: ElevenLabs

ElevenLabs remains the gold standard for AI voice acting, dubbing, and expressive dialogue without requiring expensive studio microphones.

- **Good for:** Narration, distinctive character dialogue, and emotional inflections.
- **Watch out for:** Commercial-use and voice-rights rules in the tier you choose. Always read the license terms before publishing or monetizing your film.

---

## 5. Music & Sound Design: Suno

Used to generate original scores, trailer music, and cinematic background atmosphere.

- **Watch out for:** Licensing and commercial-use terms differ between free and paid tiers. Always check the platform's current commercial rights before distributing to streaming or festivals.

---

## 6. Editing & Color Grading: DaVinci Resolve

AI generates raw clips; you still need a dedicated editor to assemble pacing, sound, and color into a cohesive movie. DaVinci Resolve is our top recommendation because its free version includes studio-grade color grading and Fairlight audio mixing.

## A Budget-Friendly Starter Stack

You do not need every tool on the market. A lean, production-ready setup requires only:
1. One script tool (Claude 3.5 Sonnet)
2. One image generator (Midjourney or Flux)
3. One video generator (Runway Gen-3 or Kling AI)
4. One audio tool (ElevenLabs)
5. One non-linear editor (DaVinci Resolve - free)

---

## How to Choose Your Toolset

- **Want story-first planning?** Look at tools built around script and storyboard.
- **Want more control over camera and motion?** Prioritize the video tools with motion brushes and explicit camera direction.
- **Short on budget?** Start with the smallest paid plan of one video tool and reuse still images across shots.
- **Publishing and monetizing?** Check commercial-use terms for every tool in your stack.
